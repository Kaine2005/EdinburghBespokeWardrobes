Bespoke sliding wardrobes in Edinburgh

Sliding-door wardrobes made to measure for your room — the space-saving choice when a hinged door would get in the way, finished to match your interior.

Space where you need it most

Hinged doors need clear floor space to swing open. In a tighter Edinburgh bedroom — where a bed, radiator or window sits close to the wardrobe wall — that space is exactly what you don't have. Sliding doors solve it. The doors move across the front of the wardrobe rather than out into the room, so you reclaim the floor.

Every sliding wardrobe we build is still fully bespoke. The track, frame and doors are made to your room's exact width and height, so the run sits flush to the walls and ceiling with no filler panels or wasted gaps.

Doors finished your way

Sliding doors are a big visual feature of the room, so the finish matters. We can frame and fill them however suits your interior:

  • Painted or sprayed panels in your chosen colour
  • Timber and veneer finishes
  • Mirror doors to open up light in a smaller room
  • Glass, or a mix of panels and mirror across the run

The same care behind the doors

It's easy to focus on the doors, but the interior is where a wardrobe earns its keep. Behind your sliding doors we plan the hanging, shelving and drawers around what you actually own, so the storage works as hard as the space allows.

If your room can take hinged doors, a fitted wardrobe gives you full-width access to the interior. Working with a recess instead? An alcove wardrobe may suit better. Send us the details of your room and we'll tell you honestly which approach fits.
