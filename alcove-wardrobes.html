Bespoke alcove wardrobes in Edinburgh

The recesses beside a chimney breast are prime storage going to waste. We turn them into built-in wardrobes that fit perfectly and look like they were always part of the room.

The space you already have, put to work

Almost every period room in Edinburgh has them — the alcoves either side of a chimney breast, or a natural nook that's too shallow for freestanding furniture. Left empty they're wasted space. Filled with a shop-bought unit they leave gaps down the sides and never quite fit.

A bespoke alcove wardrobe solves both. Built to the exact width, depth and height of the recess, it fills the space completely and sits flush to the walls, so it reads as part of the architecture rather than something added on.

Made to disappear into the room

The mark of a good alcove wardrobe is that it doesn't look like a wardrobe was added at all. We achieve that by working to the room's own details:

  • Scribed tight to uneven period walls and out-of-square recesses
  • Matched to existing skirting, cornice and picture rails
  • Painted to blend with the wall, or picked out as a feature — your choice
  • Built as a matching pair, or one wardrobe and one set of shelving

Wardrobe, shelving or both

Alcoves don't have to be identical. A common arrangement is a full wardrobe in one recess and open shelving, a desk or a media unit in the other — balanced so the room feels considered rather than crammed. We design the pair together, around how you actually use the room.

If you're fitting a whole wall rather than a recess, a fitted wardrobe is the natural step up, and sliding doors help in tighter rooms. Send us a photo of the alcove and we'll tell you what will work.
