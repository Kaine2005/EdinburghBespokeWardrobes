From first conversation to finished room

Every wardrobe follows the same clear four-stage process. No showroom pressure, no surprises — just a considered path from the idea to the fitted result.

01

Conversation

It starts with listening. Your room, your lifestyle, what you need the wardrobe to do, and what you want it to feel like.

02

Design

Drawn around your exact space. Every measurement taken on site, every detail considered, before a single cut is made.

03

Craft

Hand-built in Edinburgh. The materials, the finish, the way each joint sits flush and true. Every detail is deliberate.

04

Installation

Fitted by the people who built it. We leave your room exactly as we found it, except for the wardrobe that wasn't there before.

1 · The conversation

Everything starts with a conversation about the room and how you live in it. Where do you get dressed? What's overflowing? What do you wish the space did that it doesn't? This is also where we're honest about what will and won't work — before anyone commits to anything.

2 · Design, measured on site

We come to you and measure the room properly, accounting for uneven walls, sloping ceilings and every out-of-square corner. The design is drawn around those real dimensions, not a rough guess, and we agree the layout, finish and details together before building begins.

You'll have a clear written quote at this stage, based on what we've actually seen. No pressure, and no moving goalposts once the brief is agreed.

3 · The craft

Then it's built — by hand, in Edinburgh. The materials and joints are chosen to last, and the finish is done properly: doors that line up, joints that sit flush, a colour sprayed or painted exactly to your brief.

4 · Installation

The same person who designed and built the wardrobe fits it. We work cleanly, tidy up after ourselves, and leave your room exactly as we found it — with a wardrobe that looks like it was always meant to be there.
