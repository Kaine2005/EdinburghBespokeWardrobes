Our Craft

No two
alike.

"The wardrobe should fit the home, not the other way around."

Every home is different. The alcove you've always wanted to use. The ceiling that slopes just slightly. The room that's a foot narrower than it looks. These are the details a catalogue will never account for. We build around them.

At E.B.W, there are no models, no standard sizes, no catalogue to pick from. Each piece begins with a conversation and ends with something built for one room only. That's not a selling point. It's just how we work.

  • No two wardrobes are ever the same
  • Built to your room's exact dimensions
  • Coloured, finished, and detailed entirely to your brief
  • Crafted in Edinburgh, installed personally
How It Works

From first conversation
to finished room

01

Conversation

It starts with listening. Your room, your lifestyle, what you need the wardrobe to do, and what you want it to feel like.

02

Design

Drawn around your exact space. Every measurement taken on site, every detail considered, before a single cut is made.

03

Craft

Hand-built in Edinburgh. The materials, the finish, the way each joint sits flush and true. Every detail is deliberate.

04

Installation

Fitted by the people who built it. We leave your room exactly as we found it, except for the wardrobe that wasn't there before.
