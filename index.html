Our Craft

No two
alike.

"The wardrobe should fit the home, not the other way around."

Every home in Edinburgh is different. The alcove you've always wanted to use. The ceiling that slopes just slightly. The room that's a foot narrower than it looks. These are the details a catalogue will never account for. We build around them.

At E.B.W there are no models, no standard sizes, no showroom range to pick from. Each fitted wardrobe begins with a conversation and ends with something built for one room only. That's not a selling point. It's simply how we work.

  • No two wardrobes are ever the same
  • Built to your room's exact dimensions
  • Coloured, finished and detailed entirely to your brief
  • Crafted in Edinburgh, installed personally

How It Works

From first conversation
to finished room

01

Conversation

It starts with listening. Your room, your lifestyle, what you need the wardrobe to do, and what you want it to feel like.

02

Drawn around your exact space. Every measurement taken on site, every detail considered, before a single cut is made.

Why E.B.W

The difference is in
the approach

Fully Bespoke

There is no starting point other than your room. No template, no standard unit, no model range. Every dimension is drawn from your space, and every detail comes from your brief.

Edinburgh Made

Designed, crafted and installed locally, by the same person throughout. You're not dealing with a showroom. You work directly with the maker.

Built to Last

The materials and joints we use are chosen to hold, not to hit a price point. A wardrobe built this way should last decades.

Our Promise

What you can expect

E.B.W is a small Edinburgh workshop, so the person who quotes your project is the same person who designs, builds and fits it. Here's what that means for you.

One point of contact

No call centres, no handovers. You deal directly with the maker from the first message to the final fit.

A clear, honest quote

A written price after we've seen the space, with no pressure and no moving goalposts once we've agreed the brief.

Work we stand behind

Built to last with materials chosen for longevity, and a finish tidied and checked before we leave your home.
